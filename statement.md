Problem Statement

Many college students face difficulty in finding engaging and interactive activities during technical fests and events. Traditional events often feel repetitive, lack creativity, and fail to provide a fun and collaborative experience. There is a need for a unique, enjoyable, and learning-oriented platform that combines entertainment with participation and teamwork.

Scope of the Project

This project aims to design and organize a game-based event “Error 404: Fun Found”, which includes both technical and non-technical challenges. The event will feature multiple interactive mini-games, competitions, and a virtual currency model, encouraging active participation, competitive spirit, and creativity. It will also include a Project Expo, where students can display and promote innovative projects to attract investments using event currency.

Target Users

College students from all years and branches

Students interested in gaming, teamwork, creativity, and fun activities

Participants who want to engage in interactive college fest experiences

Visitors wanting to explore innovative projects and support teams

High-Level Features

Multiple fun and challenging game stalls

Combination of technical and non-technical tasks

Virtual UPI-style currency system for playing and earning

Investment system to support favorite project teams

Attractive rewards, prizes, and winner recognitions

Host-led activities for crowd engagement

Project Expo showcasing top ACWoC projects

Real-time scoreboard and leaderboard system
